## Query, filter and industrial limits

[HDPE_API_AUDIT.md](HDPE_API_AUDIT.md) separates static call matching from actual live requests and triages the generic analyzer's flags. The 139 successful sampled GETs and mutation validation probes are not all endpoint parameter combinations; they do not independently prove data correctness. Backend integration tests provide stronger scoped and numerical checks for the specifically asserted cases. V2's four plans cover representative event-window, period/revision, published-ledger and sample queries on a small dataset, not a production load/soak test or exhaustive index audit.

The seed's HDPE capacities, shifts, synthetic grades, stop causes, quality records and money values are explicitly illustrative. Tests validate application behavior against those assumptions; they do not certify process safety, vendor material performance, instrument calibration, commercial prices or accuracy against an operating plant. Dependency risks remain separately recorded in [HDPE_DEPENDENCY_AUDIT.md](HDPE_DEPENDENCY_AUDIT.md), regardless of functional pass counts.
